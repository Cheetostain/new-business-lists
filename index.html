What the state posts, and what you get

Every workday the state posts one text file of new companies and one of new DBAs, for all 67 counties at once. Here is part of the company file for Friday, September 25, 2026, and what we turn it into.

What we do with it, every workday

  • Read both of the day's files, companies and DBAs, the morning the state posts them.
  • Place each company in its county by ZIP code. The state's company file has no county field.
  • Keep each active filing once. Repeats and filings that are not new businesses come out.
  • Translate the codes. FLAL becomes Florida LLC, AMBR becomes Authorized Member, and people's names read first name first.
  • Tag a likely industry from the business name, and flag filings made through a filing service.
  • Sort by city and name, save a spreadsheet that opens in Excel, add a READ-ME, and email it to you.

What you pay for. The state posts these filings free at sunbiz.org, one business at a time, and in bulk as raw daily files like the one above. You pay us for the reading, sorting, cleaning and delivery. Lumora Group LLC is a private company, not affiliated with the State of Florida.

Questions

Where does the data come from?

From the public daily data files of the Florida Division of Corporations, which the state posts every workday for anyone to download (the state's daily data page). We add only three things that are not in those files: the county, from the ZIP code; the industry guess, from the business name; and a note when a known filing service filed the paperwork.

How is this different from searching sunbiz.org?

Sunbiz.org looks up one business at a time. Its ZIP code search returns every company ever registered at that ZIP, old and new, in alphabetical order and without filing dates, so it does not show you who just started. The new filings are in the state's bulk daily files, for all of Florida at once, as fixed-width text you download from the state's server and decode yourself. We do that work every workday and send you your county, sorted.

One thing the state does offer: a free search of the prior week's DBAs (fictitious names) by county. DBAs are about 1 in 7 rows in our lists; the rest are LLCs, corporations and other companies, which that search does not cover.

Are there phone numbers or email addresses?

No. The state's files do not have them and we do not add them. Each row has the business's street and mailing address as filed, which is what you need for letters, postcards and visits.

Which counties and industries can I pick?

Any of Florida's 67 counties, and any of the 11 industries listed under Prices. Need something else, like two counties or one city? Email us and we will tell you if we can do it and what it costs.

How fresh is it?

The state adds most company filings to its daily file a few days after the filing date. DBAs often show up the same day. We read each file the morning the state posts it, and your Monday list covers the files from the week before.

Can I use the list for email or phone calls?

The lists are made for mail and visits. If you find a phone number or email address elsewhere, follow the laws that apply: CAN-SPAM for email, and the Telemarketing Sales Rule, the TCPA and Florida section 501.059 for calls and texts. Never say or suggest that you are from or approved by the State of Florida. Our terms list the rules.
