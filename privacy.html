Privacy policy

Effective September 27, 2026

The short version.

  • At checkout we collect your email, your business name and the county or industry you pick.
  • Stripe handles your card. We never see or store your full card number.
  • We never sell or rent your information.
  • This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts.

1. Who we are

Lumora Group LLC is a Florida limited liability company in Gainesville, Florida. We sell lists of newly registered Florida businesses. This policy covers our customers, visitors to this site, and the businesses and people named in our lists. Contact: cottofranco@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • When you order: your email address, your business name, and the county or industry you choose. Stripe's checkout also collects your card and billing details, which Stripe processes. We see only limited payment details, such as the card brand and last four digits.
  • When you email us: your email address, your name if you include it, and your message.
  • Our records: what you bought, when, what we sent you, and any refund or cancellation.

3. How we use it

To deliver your lists and receipts, answer your questions, handle renewals, cancellations, refunds and disputes, and keep the records that tax and accounting law require. We email you only about your orders and your lists, and we do not add you to any other mailing list.

4. Who else handles it

  • Stripe, Inc. processes payments (Stripe privacy policy).
  • Google provides our email (Gmail).
  • GitHub, Inc. hosts this website. Like most hosts, it may log visitors' IP addresses for security (GitHub privacy statement).
  • Google Fonts supplies this site's typefaces. When the page loads, your browser asks Google for them, so Google receives your IP address (Google privacy policy).

We never sell or rent your information, and we share it with no one else unless the law requires it.

5. This website

No cookies, no analytics, no ads and no tracking scripts. There are no forms: the order buttons open your own email app or Stripe's checkout page.

6. How long we keep it

Order and payment records: up to 7 years, for tax and accounting. Emails: as long as we need them to serve you, then we delete them.

7. Your choices

Email us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. We reply within 1 business day and act within 30 days. We keep only what the law requires us to keep.

8. Businesses and people in our lists

Our lists come from the public filing records of the Florida Division of Corporations: business names, addresses and filing dates, and the names of owners, managers and registered agents as filed with the state. We share these only with customers who accept our terms. On this public site we show no more than business name, type, industry, city and filing date.

To be left out of future lists, email us the business name and city. We do it within 2 business days. We cannot change the state's records; for that, contact the Florida Division of Corporations.

9. Children

Our lists are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we post the new version here with a new effective date.

11. Contact

Lumora Group LLC, Gainesville, Florida. Email cottofranco@gmail.com. We answer within 1 business day, in English or Spanish.
