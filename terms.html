5. Weekly lists: renewal and cancellation

A weekly list is a monthly subscription. It renews automatically every month on the date you first paid, at the same price, until you cancel. There is no contract and no minimum term.

To cancel, email us from the address you ordered with. If your email reaches us before your renewal date, you are not charged again. If a renewal charge goes through after you asked to cancel, we refund it. You keep every list you already received.

In your first 30 days, you can ask for a refund of your first month, no questions asked. It is once per business, and a refund also cancels the subscription.

If we change a price, we email you at least 30 days before it applies to you, so you can cancel first.

8. The data

Our lists come from the public filing records of the Florida Division of Corporations, which the state also posts free at sunbiz.org. We deliver the records as the state published them ("as is"), with three additions: the county (from the ZIP code, for companies), an industry tag (a guess from words in the business name), and a note when a known filing service filed the paperwork. We do not check each record, and a mistake in the state's records carries into our lists. We do not promise any number of businesses, any sales or any other result from using a list.
